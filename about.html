About ZILD

A streetwear label built around one line: STREETWEAR

Who we are

ZILD is a clothing label focused on modern streetwear — T-shirts, hoodies, shirts, bottoms and simple accessories. The collection is kept intentionally small so every piece stands on its own: clean shapes, solid colours, and graphics that stay out of the way.

The brand runs on a simple idea: go beyond. Push past the basics, keep improving, and don't settle for the easy option.

How ZILD works

This website is the catalogue. You browse products here, look at the images, sizes, colours and descriptions, and when you find what you want you press Order on Messenger.

That opens a direct chat with the ZILD team, where your order is worked out by hand: we confirm the size, colour, availability, delivery details and payment with you personally. There is no online checkout and no account to create on this site.
